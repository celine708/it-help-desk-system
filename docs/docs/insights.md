# IT Help Desk Project: Insights

## Query 1: Open tickets by priority

**Insight:** There are 8 open tickets: 4 Low and 4 Medium priority. No Critical or High tickets are waiting, which suggests urgent issues are being handled first.

## Query 2: Average resolution time by category

**Insight:** Accounts and Passwords is the slowest category (33.6 hours on average) and also the most frequent (39 tickets). Network issues are the fastest to resolve (15.3 hours). A self-service password reset tool would remove a large share of the team's workload.

## Query 3: Overdue tickets

**Insight:** 34 resolved tickets (about 20% of 174) missed their target time. The worst cases took around 140 hours against a 72-hour target and were Low priority tickets, which suggests low-priority tickets wait too long. A reminder for tickets nearing their deadline would reduce late closures.

## Query 4: Tickets per technician

**Insight:** Nour Mansour (62) and Karim Saad (57) handle about 65% of all tickets, while Rami Haddad (33) and Maya Khoury (30) handle the rest. The workload is uneven, so reassigning some tickets or cross-training the team would balance it.

## Query 5: Open tickets past their target time

**Insight:** All 8 open tickets have already passed their target resolution time. Five of them have been open for more than 1,000 hours (over six weeks), and the oldest has been waiting about 86 days, so a backlog of low-urgency tickets is being left behind. A weekly review of open tickets would prevent this.
